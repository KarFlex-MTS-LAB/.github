<h1 align="center">KarFlex</h1>

<p align="center">
  Mobile technology for simpler vehicle rentals.
</p>

KarFlex builds practical Android experiences for discovering vehicles, booking rides, and managing rentals from one place.

## What We Build

- Vehicle browsing with clear availability and pricing
- Secure login with SMS OTP verification
- Straightforward rental and cancellation flows
- Rental history and active-rental management
- Store discovery through Google Maps

## Featured Project

### [KarFlex Android App](https://github.com/KarFlex-MTS-LAB/KarFlex)

Our Kotlin Android application uses Room/SQLite for local data, Volley for vehicle catalog retrieval, RecyclerView for lists, and Google Maps for store discovery.

## Values

- Keep the rental journey clear and fast.
- Build with maintainable Android fundamentals.
- Prefer useful details over unnecessary complexity.

## Technology

`Kotlin` `Android SDK 37` `Room` `SQLite` `Volley` `RecyclerView` `Google Maps SDK`
